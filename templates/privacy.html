1. Introduction

Presence Core LLC ("we," "our," or "us") operates Tip Tracker. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your data remains secure.

When you use Tip Tracker, we may collect the following information:

We use your information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. Your data is yours. We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

We may only disclose your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • When required by law, regulation, or legal process

5. Communications

We may send you text messages or emails only for pertinent account information, such as:

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission using HTTPS/TLS

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
